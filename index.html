About Me

Professional Summary

I am a Cloud Engineer with 2+ years of experience in designing, deploying, and managing cloud and on-premises infrastructure across multi-cloud environments — including GCP, AWS, Azure, Alibaba Cloud, Huawei Cloud, and Tencent Cloud. I specialize in building scalable, secure, and highly available systems, with a strong background in network engineering and system administration using enterprise devices like Mikrotik, Cisco, Ruijie, Fortinet, and Unifi.

Currently serving as Engineer Delivery at PT. I am experienced in DevOps practices using Ansible, Docker, and Kubernetes, with scripting proficiency in Bash and Python to improve operational efficiency and system reliability.

I continuously pursue professional growth through industry certifications, currently holding Google Associate Cloud Engineer, AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner, and Microsoft Azure Fundamentals, among many others.
